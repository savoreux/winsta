# Code of Conduct

Winsta follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best
for the people who use Winsta. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks are not accepted
in issues, pull requests, or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, [savoreux](https://github.com/savoreux), by opening an
issue marked **conduct** or by contacting the maintainer privately through GitHub. Reports are handled
promptly and confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, close issues, or block participants who
break this code of conduct.
